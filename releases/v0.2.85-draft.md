## v0.2.85 - 2026-11-19 (Draft; publication blocked)

Previous production release: [v0.2.78](https://github.com/uptaxai/uptax-desktop-releases/releases/tag/v0.2.78).
Source: [uptaxai/uptax-integrations](https://github.com/uptaxai/uptax-integrations), baseline `8e962065b039b1039dd1fbd41ff787f4a7986ca5`, candidate `42bfa73ffa1529a6d57dfc162c15e9ac1b5a4e10` ([draft source PR #265](https://github.com/uptaxai/uptax-integrations/pull/265)).

### Added

- The Drake 2025 importer carries the reviewed native client-creation, duplicate, backup, registration, and readback safeguards from the unpublished desktop candidates. Structured partnership identity can support a guarded positive K-1 Box 1 or Box 2 input when the source facts and original document corroborate it.
- A saved and verified but incomplete Drake import now enqueues one bounded `DRAKE_PARTIAL_COVERAGE` diagnostic through the existing authenticated desktop queue. It records stage `coverage` and the number of held import notes without taxpayer identity or transfer contents.
- The installer retains the published ATX, ProSeries, Lacerte, CCH, and Drake resources and the reviewed current bundled UpTax interface.

### Fixed

- A partial Drake import no longer disappears from the failure alert path. A stale native `error.txt` or `stage.txt=done` cannot replace its coverage diagnostic; a later exception remains an uncertain outcome.
- The customer sees that the return was saved and must be inspected in Drake before another Send. The partial count reflects held notes rather than claiming an exact number of tax values.

### Changed or removed

- No tax year, supported return type, integration, or packaged mapping file was intentionally removed. The reviewed Drake additions concern 2025 Form 1040 input coverage; they do not establish complete calculated-form parity. Other integrations retain their published package coverage.
- No public update channel has been changed. The EXE is a local candidate only.
